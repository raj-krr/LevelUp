How It Works

From Photo to Guided Understanding

Three simple steps designed to empower parents, never cheat for students:

📸

1. Snap Homework

Photograph any printed question, worksheet, or child's handwritten notebook page (Grades 3–8).

💡

2. Understand in Your Language

Gemma 4 explains the concept in your mother tongue using relatable cultural analogies (rotis, cricket, water pumps).

🪜

3. Coach with Socratic Hints

Follow conversational parent scripts, hints, and worked solutions so your child learns to solve it independently.
