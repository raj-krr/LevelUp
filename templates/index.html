The Philosophy

Built for Parents. Never for Cheating.

How LevelUp bridges the educational divide in multilingual Indian homes:

🗣️

Overcoming Language Barrier

Schools teach in English or formal textbooks, but parents think and teach best in their mother tongue. LevelUp translates concepts into 6 Indic languages so parents feel confident and empowered.

🚫

Zero Direct Answers

Standard AI solvers (ChatGPT, Photomath) encourage mindless copying. LevelUp never blurts out the answer. Instead, it provides conversational scripts for parents and progressive Socratic nudges for the child.

💡

Everyday Cultural Metaphors

Abstract formulas are anchored in relatable experiences: dividing rotis at the dinner table, calculating cricket run rates, and visualizing electricity through village water pumps.

✍️

"Check Child's Work" Diagnostician

Parents often can't tell if multi-step homework is right. Gemma 4 reviews handwritten notebook pages, celebrates what went well, and pinpoints where confusion started.

🚀 Model 1: Multimodal Explainer

gemma-4-26b-a4b-it extracts visual homework context, reasons about grade-appropriate curriculum levels, and translates explanations into natural regional Indic phrasing.

🛡️ Model 2: Pedagogical Verifier

gemma-4-31b-it audits the output to ensure mathematical accuracy, verifies that no direct answers were leaked, and flags potential hallucinations.

🔒 Privacy by Design

Images are read directly into memory via BytesIO, processed, and immediately discarded. No child photos or handwriting samples are ever written to disk or recorded in server logs.

⚡ Lightweight & Open-Weights

Leverages Google DeepMind's open Gemma 4 weights, enabling transparent, auditable, and low-latency educational intelligence accessible to everyone.
